I like finding the common structure in problems that seem unrelated. Bonus points if I can draw it as a commutative diagram.

A Steerable Deep Network for Model-Free Diffusion MRI Registration

Gianfranco Cortés, X. Qu, B. C. Vemuri

We formulate deformable registration directly in the acquisition space of raw dMRI. We show how 3D steerable convolutions can serve as spatio-angular feature extractors and use them to build a registration network à la VoxelMorph.

Higher Order Gauge Equivariant Convolutions for Neurodegenerative Disorder Classification

Gianfranco Cortés, Y. Yu, R. Chen, M. Armstrong, D. Vaillancourt, B. C. Vemuri

We propose a two-part architecture for classifying neurodegenerative disorders from raw dMRI: a 3D convolutional kernel network extracts spatial features across voxels, and a gauge-equivariant Volterra network extracts angular features within each voxel.

A Machine Learning Approach to the Nirenberg Problem

Gianfranco Cortés, M. Esteban-Casadevall, Y. Feng, J. Henkel, E. Hirst, T. S. Gherardini, A. G. Stapleton

We use a PINN to search for metrics on the sphere, conformal to the round metric, that realize a prescribed Gaussian curvature. Our experiments provide numerical evidence for and against existence in several open cases. This project began at LOGML 2025.

Experience

Graduate Research Assistant at CVGMI Lab, University of Florida2021–presentgeometric deep learningmedical image analysisdiffusion MRIdeformable registration

Advisor: Professor Baba C. Vemuri

  • Proposed Covariant Diffusion MRI (CoDi), a geometric framework that models the acquisition space as a cotangent bundle of the scanner space to make dMRI’s symmetries and transformation behavior explicit.
  • As part of the CoDi initiative, brought to the forefront a lesser-known equivalence between spatio-angular convolution and 3D steerable convolution, pioneering its use to build reusable layers for geometry-aware backbones for data-driven dMRI analysis.
  • Built a steerable network for deformable registration that aligns raw multi-shell dMRI scans in their native acquisition space without first fitting a diffusion model at each voxel. On 10 held-out HCP scan pairs, the method achieved a mean Dice score of 0.781 across 22 brain structures, versus 0.758 for ANTs SyN on fractional anisotropy maps.
Research Intern at Riverain TechnologiesSummer 20243D segmentationchest CTdomain shift

Mentors: Jason Knapp, Adam Harrison, and Xiaotian (Dennis) Wu

  • Trained a 3D SegResNet for pulmonary artery–vein separation in chest CT, segmenting mediastinal arterial and venous structures extending just beyond the lung hila to provide anchors for downstream intrapulmonary vessel tracking.
  • Addressed pediatric-to-adult domain shift between ImageCHD and LIDC chest CT datasets by correcting noisy vessel labels, adding anatomical classes to constrain segmentation, and modulating contrast across cardiovascular structures.
